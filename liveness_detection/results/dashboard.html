Protocol & interpretation

Five grouped outer folds hold each subject out once for testing. A separate set of outer-training subjects is used for validation; the remaining subjects are used for training. Grouped cross-validation, scaling, and hyperparameter selection use training subjects only. The validation subject selects the fixed operating threshold. Class 0 is genuine/live; class 1 is spoof/attack. FAR equals APCER and FRR equals BPCER under these definitions. All metrics and plots are based on independent held-out test predictions.

These local results do not establish official benchmark performance or real-world security.

Auxiliary client split not evaluated: The adaptation list does not contain both bona fide and attack labels. The local RGB test images do not cover every annotated test record.
